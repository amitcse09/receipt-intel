# PaisaBack Rewards — Cashback Rates

## Rates by membership tier
Silver members earn 1% cashback, Gold members earn 2%, and Platinum members earn 3% on the eligible receipt amount. Tier is determined by total eligible spend in the previous 12 months: Gold from ₹50,000 and Platinum from ₹2,00,000.

## Partner store bonus
Purchases at partner stores earn an additional 2% on top of the tier rate. Current partner stores are Nilgiri Mart, FreshKart and Sahyadri Electronics. The partner bonus applies to both in-store and online orders from these partners.

## Caps
Cashback is capped at ₹500 per receipt and ₹3,000 per member per calendar month, including any partner bonus. Amounts above the cap are not carried forward.

## Payout
Cashback is credited to the PaisaBack wallet. Wallet balance can be withdrawn to a bank account by UPI once it reaches ₹100. Withdrawals are free and processed within 2 business days.
