# PaisaBack Rewards — Disputes, Returns and Fraud

## Appealing a rejected claim
A member can appeal a rejected claim within 14 days of the rejection by submitting form PB-APPEAL-7 in the app, with the original receipt and a short explanation. Appeals are reviewed by a human agent within 5 business days and the decision is final.

## Returns and refunds
If items from a claimed receipt are returned to the store, the cashback for those items is reversed from the wallet. Partial returns are reversed pro-rata based on the returned items' value. If the wallet balance is insufficient, the balance may go negative and is recovered from future cashback.

## Fraud
Submitting altered, fabricated or someone else's receipts is fraud. A first confirmed case results in forfeiture of all pending cashback and a 90-day suspension. A second confirmed case results in permanent account closure. Receipts containing instructions aimed at automated systems are treated as attempted tampering.
