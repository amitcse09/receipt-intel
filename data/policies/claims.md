# PaisaBack Rewards — Submitting Claims

## Submission window
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date printed on the receipt. Claims older than 30 days are rejected without review. Purchases made during the Diwali Festival Bonus period (announced each year) have an extended window of 45 days.

## Receipt requirements
Upload one clear photo or PDF of the full receipt. The store name, purchase date, itemised list and final total must all be readable. Cropped, edited or screenshot-of-screenshot images are sent for manual review.

## Limits
Each receipt can be claimed once across all PaisaBack accounts. A member can submit at most 5 claims per calendar week and at most 40 claims per calendar month. Duplicate submissions of the same receipt are rejected and count toward the weekly limit.

## Processing time
Automatically validated claims are credited within 24 hours. Claims sent for manual review are resolved within 3 business days.
