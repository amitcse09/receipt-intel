# PaisaBack Rewards — Purchase Eligibility

## Eligible purchases
Cashback is earned on purchases of groceries, household goods, personal care, apparel and electronics made at any physical retail store in India. Online orders are eligible only from partner stores listed in the Cashback Rates policy.

## Excluded categories
The following items never earn cashback, even when they appear on an otherwise eligible receipt: alcohol, tobacco and vaping products, lottery tickets, gift cards and vouchers, fuel, prescription medicines, and government fees or taxes paid separately. Excluded items are removed from the receipt total before cashback is calculated.

## Minimum receipt value
A receipt must total at least ₹200 after excluded items are removed. Receipts below ₹200 are not eligible and the claim is closed automatically.

## Who can claim
Members must be 18 or older with a verified mobile number. Business purchases with a GST invoice in a company name are not eligible; only personal purchases qualify.
